    Tips

    Ideas that work in every layout
    • New socket, bottom-right corner. It sits inside the last wardrobe section in every layout. Cut a hole in the Pax back panel and use it for an LED strip on top of the wardrobe, or to charge a vacuum out of sight.
    • Hinged doors, not sliding. Pax with sliding doors is 66 cm deep, which cuts the walkway at the foot of the bed from 62 to 54 cm.
    • Build up, not along. The 236 cm Pax holds about 17% more than the 201 cm one. You need a ceiling of roughly 240 cm to stand the frames up.
    • Get things off the floor. A wall-mounted desk and bedside shelf show floor underneath them.
    • Keep the middle empty. One rug between the desk and the bed and nothing else.
